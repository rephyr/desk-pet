errands (picked: A, the corkboard; built)

Safe idle work: tap a resting pet, then a job (or use + and −). More pets = faster, meters pay each time they fill. Time runs 6x fast here.

A: corkboardEach job is a sticky note; the crew are polaroids pinned to it. Resting pets wait in a shoebox. A with thousands of petsPast 6 pets a job shows a pile of polaroids, the count and a bustling crowd. + and − move 1, 10, 100 or all; "share them out" spreads the resting pets. B: little townEach job is a tiny building on a crayon map; the crew bob by its door. Tap one for its card. Resting pets sit on a bench. C: quiltEach job is a sewn patch; the meter is a thread stitching its way around the edge. Resting pets in the sewing basket.

errands with upgrades (picked: A, the pegboard; built)

Errands open after "unstick the flap"; the basket comes with a sleepy pet in it. Coins buy the pets better tools, jobs level up, levels reach goals (stars): the lemonade stand, x2 coins. A find is worth a few capsules, so machine upgrades raise errands too. Tap things to buy; the coins a minute go up.
