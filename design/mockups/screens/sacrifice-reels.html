the plushie machine

F1/F2 mockup. Pick the keeper, feed pets into the hopper, spin: one reel per part, each lands on an upgrade, a blank or a crack. Bank a reel to keep what it holds (that reel stops), or hold it: the next upgrade on it counts double, a crack takes the held ones away. Misses puff stuffing, stuffing buys nudges (the little tab on a reel pulls the one above down) and holds. Every frame is playable: pull the lever or press spin. The fed pet's rarity = spins, its finish = nudges, "lucky" = fewer cracks. Numbers are placeholders.
