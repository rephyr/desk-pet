the wish list

F3 on the book. Tap a sticker you've found to wish for it, pick a shelf, send 1 / 10 / 100 / all. Steps of 200, 600, 2k, 6k. Numbers are placeholders.

Look A: the wishing jara jar stands beside the book; pets hop in and fill it in their colours, a star lights on its side for each full step; the wished sticker is the jar's label.
Look B: the wish kiteits own bookmark in the book: pick a sticker on the left page, pets climb the string on the right; each full step ties a bow on the kite's tail.
Look C: the dandelionsa strip sewn under the book: four dandelions, pets are the fluff; a full one blows away with a sparkle and the next opens.
