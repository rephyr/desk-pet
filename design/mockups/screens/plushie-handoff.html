the plushie machine: your pet's rules

F2's handoff. Later in the game a pet spins the plushie machine for you, by rules you give it: "bank at 3, never risk the body". It plays worse than you (slower, and it only nudges and holds the way its rules say). The keeper here is gold dragon (body 4, palette 2, pattern 1, eyes 3, accessory full); lilac blob, your pet, spins. Each frame plays itself: change a rule and watch it.

Look A: the rules card

The machine as built; your pet stands on a stool at the lever. The side card turns into its rules: who spins (you | lilac blob), then sentences with steppers, like the sorting rule.

Look B: tags on the reels

The rules hang on the machine: a paper tag under every reel ("bank at", a heart for "careful") and one on the lever for nudges. A rule per part, so the body can be careful and the pattern greedy.

Look C: a job on the automation tab

Spinning is one more job card for your pet (one job at a time, like the crank). Its rules are its own notebook page, in its crayon.
