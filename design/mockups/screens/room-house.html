room upgrades: the house card

Tap the room pill on the bookcase and the house card opens under it. Buy the next step and watch the house grow (the pill grows too, pets trickle in). Tap outside or ✕ to close, the pill to open again. Numbers are placeholders.

Look A: the dollhouseone house that grows, the next step drawn in pencil inside it, one "next up" row with the price under it.
Look B: the flip booka page per step: flip back through what you built (a "moved in!" stamp on each), the next page has the price; the wisp steps get a coral ribbon.
Look C: the house-shaped cardthe card is the house: a floor per step with a price tag on a nail, it gets taller as you build; wisp steps squeeze into floors you already have.
