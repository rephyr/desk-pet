encounter art

When your pets stop at something on the trail, it shows up just ahead of them, to the right, while the adventure card asks what to do. Four samples (the basket, the fox, the glowing mushrooms, the humming chest) in three looks.

A: pixel things on the trail

Drawn in the same chunky pixels as the pets, standing on the path like they're really there. Glowing things glow. Easiest to read next to the pets.

B: crayon doodles

Wobbly crayon lines like the map's place doodles. Softer and more storybook, a bit less "there".

C: polaroid on a stick

The pixel thing on a little taped photo stuck in the ground, with its name under it. Reads like a postcard or a sign, and it matches the stickers and postcards.
