knacks

D1: every part has a named knack. Its size comes from the part's rarity, and your active pet's knacks boost everything. First the proposed list (all 35 parts from data/parts.json), then three looks of how knacks show in the collectibles tab. Numbers are placeholders for data/knacks.json.

the proposal

Each slot leans one way, and each part has its own knack. Size = the knack's step x the rarity:

Look Aa knack list: the details sticker lists each part with its knack and number, the grid cards stay as they are
Look Btags on a string: knacks hang on a line of pegged tags under the pet, and each grid card wears its best knack as a little tag
Look Cbadges: knacks are round sewn-on badges, tap one to read it, the parts list stays, grid cards get their best badge in the corner
