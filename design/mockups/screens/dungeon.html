the old well, all the way down

E1, the dungeon page in adventures. Every frame plays: step the orders and the herd, then press "down we go!" and watch the landings light up. Numbers are placeholders (floor strength 100 x 1.2^n against the army's power, lanterns ~1.15^floor x pets sent).

Look A: the cross-sectionthe well is a tall drawn column you scroll down, the army in the middle, the orders card and the last trip in the side column.
Look B: the well is the pageone drawn scene: the army waits on the grass by the well mouth with the whole well under them; orders and the herd sit in the side column.
Look C: the orders letterthe orders are a big letter from your pet, the army lines up under it, the well is a thin map line of floors down the right edge.
