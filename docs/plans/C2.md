# C2: past the edge + the little school (look A) - build plan

From picks.md (C2/F3 in brainstorm 2, look picks round 2) and the mockup
`past-the-edge.html` look A. Builds on C1's herd (counts per rarity x finish).

## 1. The edge (adventures tab, beyond-the-fence page)

**When it shows (hidden until then):** unlock `edge` opens `feature:edge` when every place on the
beyond page is open (new earn key `all_places: "beyond"`), or earlier through a new rumour `edge`
("a spot where the map just stops", requires `page:beyond` + `location:orchard`). While it's only a
rumour, the signpost is drawn like other rumour spots (tap = say yes).

**Map (MapView, page beyond, edge open):** the paper stops ~110 px short of the right side and gets
a torn right edge; `_fit` uses the narrower paper. Under the tear, a second sheet peeks out: crayon
scribbles in the colours of pets sent (one scribble per sent pet, up to `marks_max`, spread by
seed) and a big "N" + "to go". No outline of what's coming. A crayon signpost sits on the tear with
the label "the edge" and a hotspot; tapping it emits `edge_picked`.

**Edge card (EdgeCard):** stuck at the top of the right column above "away" (as in look A):
sign icon + "the edge", shelf rows (a row per rarity that has resting herd pets: face, rarity,
count; tap picks it), 1 / 10 / 100 / all. Only herd counts, never cards. "all" = min(resting on
that shelf, to go). Pets fly off (small tween of a few faces), each one adds a night-sky star, the
sheet redraws. Your pet always says the same cheer: "they'll draw the rest of the map!".

**Full:** the page's count reaches `need` -> `GameState.edge_done("next_door")` is true ->
`check_unlocks` opens the unlock entry `next_door` (`opens: ["page:next_door"]`,
`earn: { "edge": "next_door" }`). That is the ONE hook for the next-door lane. After the last page
in data is full, the tucked sheet and signpost go away (the tear stays): nothing is teased.

**Rules:** `scripts/adventure/edge.gd` (Edge, pure): `fresh()`, `page(catalog, i)`, `need`,
`to_go(state)`, `add(state, n, palettes)`. GameState: `edge_shown()`, `edge_to_go()`,
`send_past_edge(rarity, n) -> int` (takes from `resting_herd()` of that rarity, plainest finish
first, via a new `Collection.send_off(key, n)`: takes n off the count, adds n stars, stores palettes
of up to 64 stand-in faces per call, the rest only counted like C1's `fallen_n`).

## 2. The little school (automation tab)

**When it shows:** unlock `school` opens `feature:school` at `earn: { "edge_sent": 100, "open":
"tab:automation" }` (placeholder: after the first 100 pets past the edge, so school comes second).
Popup "new: the little school!" goes to automation.

**Switch:** `your pet | workers | school` (school only once open; workers only when it exists, as
now). No whistle yet (other step).

**Page (SchoolView, look A):** left = the classroom (custom draw): a chalkboard with a teacher
pet, "class 4" and the chalk "+3.2%" (the step this class would give now, blank when empty);
24 desks (6 x 4) filling in proportion to the class (faces in the class's rarity mix); a
"teachers" row of finished classes (newest 4 groups: 3 faces, size, "+3.0%"). Right = side card:
school icon + "the little school", "every worker x1.10", "class 4" + "163 of 450" + meter, the same
shelf rows + 1/10/100/all (clamped to seats left), and "ring the bell" at the bottom: disabled until
the class is full, then glowing (primary + pulse). YOU ring it: confetti, the class becomes a
teachers group, its pets become stars, the next class starts, the x number bumps. Cheer never
changes: "class is in! everybody sit nicely."; full: "everyone's here! ring the bell!"; bell:
"ding ding! the new teachers are so proud.".

**Rules:** `scripts/idle/school.gd` (School, pure): `fresh()`, `class_size(catalog, i)` (sizes
list, then x grow), `step(catalog, seated)` = base + per_point x average stand points (mockup:
1 + 1.4 x avg), `boost(state)` = product of (1 + step/100) over classes. GameState:
`seat_in_school(rarity, n) -> int` (off the herd count into `school.seated`, so they stop counting
for the room / resting), `class_full()`, `ring_bell() -> bool`, `school_boost()`.
**Where the boost goes:** `workers_speed(id)` (machine + box workers) and errand crews' rate
(the `_job_tools` speed factor), one helper `school_boost()` so B2's `boost("workers")` can take it
over at the merge.

  marks: [palettes, up to marks_max] }`

  `Herd.clean_counts`, clamps steps and sizes.

## Dev steps + flows

- DevDriver: `edge-send <rarity> <n>`, `seat <rarity> <n>`, `bell`, `classes <n>`,
  `map-page <id>`.
- `tests/flows/edge.flow`: new save, beyond + all places open, herd 3000 commons + some rares,
  adventures, map-page beyond, shot (tear + signpost + 500 to go), click the edge, shot card,
  click 100, shot scribbles, all, wait popup (next door), shot, `expect fits` at every stop.
  Plus a rumour-only shot.
- `tests/flows/school.flow`: unlock school, herd, automation, click school, shot empty class,
  seat 40 mixed, shot (bell glowing), click ring the bell, shot teachers row + x number, seat into
  class 2, 1M herd shot, `expect fits` each time.
