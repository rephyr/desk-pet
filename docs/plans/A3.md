# A3 More errand jobs: build plan

Three new jobs on the corkboard, each opened by a goal on another job's level (like the lemonade
stand): **savings jar**, **kitchen**, **scouting**. Same one-formula meter as every job; each
adds one small twist in data. Numbers are placeholders for tools/balance.gd.

## The chain

```
coin hunt lv 10 -> lemonade stand (as now)
lemonade lv 10  -> savings jar      (the goal keeps its x2 and adds the jar)
coin hunt lv 25 -> kitchen          (the goal keeps its x2 and adds the kitchen)
savings jar lv 10 -> scouting       ("a later goal": my pick, see questions)
```

## The three jobs

**Savings jar**: fills slowly, pays one big chunk.
- `seconds` 1800 (30 min with one pet), `pay` { capsules: 100 }, its own `crew_power` 0.5 (the
  global one is 0.8), so extra pets barely speed it up and it always stays slow. One pet in the
  jar earns a bit more than one pet on the coin hunt. A big crew earns less than on the coin hunt.
- stat luck, traits greedy 1.2, lazy 1.1 (lazy pets are good at sitting by a jar).
- Tools (ids `bigger_jar`, `slot`): **a bigger jar** (+10 capsules' worth a chunk, 6000 coins, x1.2, no max), **a wider
  slot** (fills 8% sooner, 9000, x1.3, max 25).
- Goals: lv 10 scouting opens, lv 25 x2, lv 50 x3.
- The note says "about 7.4k coins when full" and "full in 24m 10s" instead of "every ...". When it
  pays, the "+7.4k" pop is bigger and gold, and there's a little coin burst. It never turns into
  the flowing stream.

**Kitchen**: brings nothing, makes every other job a bit faster, feeds your pet.
- data: `"kitchen": { "most": 0.3, "half": 2 }`. Speed bonus = most x cooks / (cooks + half),
  where cooks = crew size x their average speed: 1 cook +10%, 2 +15%, 4 +20%, 10 +25%, never
  past +30%.
- **Never beats real jobs**: the bonus is also capped at crew_power x cooks / pets on the other
  jobs, which is about what those cooks would add on a real job. With a few pets the soft curve
  wins. With big crews the cap wins and the kitchen fades to a snack stand. It still helps the jobs
  that don't bring coins (jar, scouting, scrapyard), and it feeds your pet.
- The bonus adds to every other job's speed (like snack break), not to the kitchen itself.
- `seconds` 600, `pay` { meal: 25 }: each full meter feeds your active pet +25 food (+3 mood).
- stat power, traits hungry 1.3 ("hungry pets cook best"), lazy 0.9. No tools, so no level, no
  goals and no pegboard shelf.
- The note says "every job 12% faster" (live) and "a meal every 10m". Its pop says "+ a meal".
  Sometimes your pet says a yum line.

**Scouting**: raises the spot and rumour chance of your next trips. Chance only: the map's ?
clouds stay a surprise.
- `seconds` 900, `pay` { note: 1 }: each full meter is one **scout note**. You can hold 2 notes
  (`"scout": { "hold": 2, "spot": 0.15, "rumour_x": 1.5 }`). When you hold the most you can, the
  meter stays full and waits.
- A trip **you** send takes one note if its place still has something to find (a lead not yet
  spotted, or a rumour you could still hear). That trip gets +0.15 on every lead's spot chance and
  x1.5 on its rumour chances. Dungeons never take notes. Auto parties don't take notes either (see
  questions).
- stat speed, traits curious 1.2, zoomy 1.2.
- Tools (ids `map_case`, `glasses`): **a map case** (+1 note held, 15000, x2, max 3), **field glasses** (notes come 8%
  sooner, 20000, x1.3, max 20). No goals.
- The note shows "notes ready 1 / 2". A trip that took a note gets a tiny note doodle on its away
  card, and the pet on the trip says a line at set-off. No odds are shown anywhere.

## Files

Changed
- `data/errands.json`: 3 jobs (jar, kitchen, scouting) with doodles and colours; new keys:
  per-job `crew_power`, `kitchen`, `scout`, pay kinds `meal` and `note`, tool effect `hold`;
  `share: false` on kitchen and scouting; the lemonade lv 10 and coin hunt lv 25 goals get a `text`
  next to their `x`. The _note is updated.
- `data/unlocks.json`: `jar` (job:jar, job_level lemonade 10), `kitchen` (job:kitchen, coin_hunt
  25), `scouting` (job:scouting, jar 10), each with a popup ("new: a savings jar!" ...) and an
  announce line.
- `data/voice.json`: errands_meal, errands_jar_full, trip_scouted lines.
- `scripts/idle/jobs.gd`: `pay()` handles meal and note; `rate()` uses the job's own crew_power;
  `goal_words()` says "x2 tips and a savings jar opens" when a goal has both; new
  `kitchen_bonus(job, cooks, others, crew_power)`.
- `scripts/game_state.gd`: `scout_notes` + `scout_hold()`; `kitchen_bonus()` (cached, and cleared
  when crews change: `_crews_changed` must now clear `_job_tools` too); `job_rate` adds it for
  every job but the kitchen; `_work_for` turns meal into food and note into notes, and skips
  scouting while the notes are full; `send_on_adventure` sets `run.scouted`; `_spot_places`
  passes the bonus; `share_out` / `_auto_place` skip jobs with `share: false`. The offline
  catch-up drops food **before** the errands catch-up runs (it's after now), so meals made while
  you're away count.
- `scripts/adventure/intel.gd`: `roll(..., bonus := 0.0)` adds the bonus to each lead.
- `scripts/adventure/run_state.gd`: `scouted` (bool, saved, default false).
- `scripts/adventure/adventure_runner.gd`: rumour rewards' chance x rumour_x when `state.scouted`
  (both roll sites, 141 and 265).
- `scripts/ui/errands_tab.gd`: pay lines for the three, the jar's big pop, "+ a meal" / "+ a note"
  pops; waiting notes only for the job that is **the next goal** of an open job (so at most one
  per job, never a row of locked notes).
- `scripts/ui/errand_tools_view.gd`: the same next-goal rule for waiting shelves.
- The adventures away card: the tiny note doodle for `run.scouted`.
- `scripts/ui/ui_theme.gd`: doodles job_jar, job_kitchen, job_scout; icons tool_jar, tool_slot,
  tool_case, tool_glasses.
- `scripts/dev/dev_driver.gd`: `job <id> <n>` (n resting pets onto a job), `notes <n>` (hold n
  scout notes).
- `tools/balance.gd`: skip jobs that don't pay coins or parts. Add a jar line and a kitchen line
  (the bonus for 1/2/4/10 cooks with 10/100/1000 pets elsewhere).
- Docs: CLAUDE.md session note, docs/dev-plan.md (A3 built), docs/design.md errands part.

New
- `tests/flows/errand_jobs.flow`.

## Save

SAVE_VERSION 22 -> 23: new field `"scout_notes"` (int, clamped 0..hold). RunState gets
`"scouted"`. New job crews and fills go in the existing `jobs` dict, and the new tool levels go in
the existing `errand_tools`. No migration: old saves start with 0 notes and the jobs closed until
their goals are reached. A save already past a goal gets the job on its next check_unlocks.

## Tests (tests/test_core.gd, in _test_jobs / _test_errand_tools)

- The three jobs exist with name, seconds, pay; each `needs` opens through a job_level unlock that
  matches a goal's `at` on the job it waits for.
- Jar: its crew_power is used (4 pets < 4 x 1 pet's rate by more than on the coin hunt); one pet
  in the jar beats one pet on the coin hunt per hour, and 5 pets don't.
- Kitchen: bonus 1/2/4/10 cooks = 0.10/0.15/0.20/0.25, never past `most`; with 1000 pets elsewhere
  the cap makes it tiny; it isn't applied to the kitchen itself; meal fills add food, capped at 100.
- Scouting: notes stop at hold (and hold + map case levels); a sent trip takes one, a dungeon trip
  and an auto trip don't; `Intel.roll` with the bonus spots more over many seeded rolls; rumour
  chance goes x1.5 on a scouted run; RunState round trip keeps `scouted`.
- goal_words with x + text reads "x2 tips and a savings jar opens".
- Share out never puts pets in the kitchen or on scouting.
- Save round trip keeps scout_notes; a v22 save loads with 0.
- Also run tools/balance.gd and the flows errands, errand_tools, errands_crowd, fits, postcard.

## Questions for Emilia (smallest safe pick for now)

1. Scouting opens at **savings jar lv 10** ("a later goal"). Other choices: lemonade lv 25, or a
   trips count.
2. **Auto parties don't use scout notes**, only trips you send. Otherwise your pet's auto
   adventures would eat them all at once.
3. Kitchen "never beats real jobs": at big crews the cap makes it almost nothing for coins. It
   stays useful for feeding your pet and for the jobs that don't bring coins. Is that right, or
   should it keep a small floor (e.g. always at least +5%)?
4. Kitchen and scouting are skipped by "share them out" and by your pet's auto sharing: you staff
   them yourself.
5. Scout notes are held at 2 (up to 5 with the map case), and a trip only takes one if its place
   still has something to spot or hear.
6. The jar has no bonus for time away (it just runs like every job). A "pays extra if you leave it
   alone" twist was left out on purpose.
7. All prices, times and pays are placeholders until balance.gd tunes them.
