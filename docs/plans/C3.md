# C3: busy paws + the new homes stall + the sorting rule: build plan

Sources: dev-plan C3 steps 1-2 + the room irritant, picks.md (C3 look A the stall, C1/C3 points
and keeps, stars only for pets that leave), mockup design/mockups/screens/new-homes.html look A.
Builds on C1 + the herd (this lane, save v23).

## 1. Busy paws: "new pets join here" per job

- Replaces the one global `jobs_auto` switch ("<pet> shares out new pets") with a switch on each
  job: every errand note, and each worker job's side card (machines, box tables; not adventures:
  parties keep their slots and "fill up" stays for them).
- New pets (box openings, gifts, pets home from adventures: the same arrivals jobs_auto placed)
  go to jobs whose switch is on: worker jobs with free spots first (best workers, like "fill up"),
  the rest spread over switched-on errands, smallest crew first (`_auto_place` with a job list).
  No switch on = they rest, as today.
- Shows when the old switch showed (`spare_count() > STEPS_AFTER`), as a small CheckButton
  "new pets join here" on the note / card. The "share them out" button stays.
- Your old active pet going back to work: only if some job has its switch on.

## 2. The new homes stall (look A)

- **Where:** pets page of collectibles. Once it's open the page splits: the bookcase on the left
  (1fr), a 252 px side column on the right with the stall and, later, the rule card. Before it
  opens the bookcase keeps the full width (hidden until earned).
- **Opens:** the first time the room is full (the irritant first, then the relief). Unlock entry
  `new_homes` -> `feature:new_homes`, earn `{"room": "full"}` backed by a saved `room_was_full`
  flag set in `open_boxes` / `_workers_open` when the room stops an opening. No popup text; your
  pet says a new line once ("a stall! for new homes").
- **Picking a shelf:** tapping a plank picks it for the stall (dashed pink border like the
  mockup); tapping the picked plank again opens the shelf (ShelfView) as before. Inside an open
  shelf the stall stays and takes from that shelf. The cushion's MiniCards still open shelves.
- **The stall (`NewHomesStall`):** striped awning drawn in `_draw` (pink seam / pale stripes with
  scallops), title "new homes" with a house doodle, a "from" row (shelf's pet face, rarity name
  in its colour, how many can go), four buttons 1 / 10 / 100 / all ("all" with a pink seam), the
  box jar (box doodle, lilac meter, "14/25") and the box pile under it (3 stacked box doodles +
  count + "starter boxes", the pile is `bag[box]`).
- **Who can go (by hand):** the shelf's pets that aren't always-cards and aren't busy: herd counts
  and free plain cards, any rarity. Order: normal finish before shiny; resting before working
  (then off errands, then off machines/tables, like `_herd_off_places`); herd counts first, then
  the oldest free plain cards (the newest 20 go last). Favourites, the active
  pet, new-part pets, holo+ cards, pets away, pinned pulls never go by hand here.
- **Pays:** points toward a box: worth per rarity in data (common 1, uncommon 2, rare 5, epic 12,
  legendary 20, mythic 40), 25 points = 1 box onto the pile. The jar keeps the leftover.
  A "+1" rises from the jar and the box doodle pops when a box drops.
- **Cheer:** every take, your pet says "they'll have a big garden!" (one line, never changes).
- **Stars:** every pet that leaves (by hand or by rule) adds a night-sky star: new
  `Collection.leave(counts, cards)` takes them off in one go, adds `n` stars (palettes: cards
  their own, counts a stand-in face's, capped by fallen_keep as today), emits `pets_left(n)`
  (NightSky redraws on it) plus `pets_removed` / `herd_changed` as needed. Never explained.

## 3. The sorting rule card

- **Turns up** after enough clearing by hand: unlock entry `sorting` -> `feature:sorting`, earn
  `{"homes_by_hand": 300}` (placeholder, in data). Tilted index card under the stall.
- **Card (`SortingCard`, Tilted ~-1.5):** head "sorting" + off | on segmented. Line: "new pets
  below ‹rare› go to ‹new homes›" with steppers (‹ › buttons, never dropdowns): below = uncommon /
  rare / epic / legendary / mythic (only rarities you've had), go to = new homes / work. Keeps row
  (chips): "✦ ‹holo› and up" (finish stepper: shiny / holo / ghost / glitch / prismatic, only
  finishes you've seen), "new parts", "favourites" (fixed chips, always kept). Off look: lines
  dimmed. Off by default.
- **What it does:** only box openings sort (by hand, your pet, box workers, the machine's pet
  box; gifts never). Sorting happens as pets arrive, so only pets pulled after it's on are
  touched. `Collection.add(pets, sorter)`: after the book counts a pet (so `new_part` is known)
  and before `pets_added` / refold, the sorter picks pets with rarity below the line, finish below
  the keep, not new_part. New homes: they leave at once (stars, points; they never take room).
  Work: they go to switched-on jobs (busy paws), or spread over every open errand if none is on.
  The reveal still shows what you pulled.
- **"sorted today N":** dashed tag on each plank below the line while the rule is on, and on the
  card. Counts rule-sorted pets since local midnight (saved with the day).
- The rule doesn't free room ahead: `open_boxes` still clamps to room_left before rolling
  (sorted pets leave right after, so the room stays free). Smallest safe first version.

## Flow `new_homes` + dev steps

Dev steps: `homes <rarity> <n|all>` (the stall takes), `rule on|off [below] [to] [keep]`,
`join <job> on|off`, `homes-points <n>`.
Flow (from rich, view full, each view `expect fits` + shot):
1. herd + fill-room, a box waits -> stall opens: `shot stall_open`.
2. pick the common plank, take 10 / 100 / all: jar fills, boxes drop, stars, cheer: `shot taken`.
3. take 300 more by hand -> rule card shows: `shot rule_card`; switch on, steppers: `shot rule_on`.
4. give boxes, open 10 with the rule on: sorted today ticks, commons gone: `shot sorted`.
5. rule to "work", `join coin_hunt on`, open boxes: they land on the coin hunt: `shot work`.
6. errands tab + automation workers with the join switches: `shot join_errands`, `shot join_workers`.
7. an open shelf with the stall beside it: `shot shelf_stall`. A million commons: `shot million`.
Rerun all flows (fits, pets_shelves, errands, workers, automation, boxes, pet_box, tutorial...).
